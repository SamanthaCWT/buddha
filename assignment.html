Electricity

Electricity[Note:Electricityis a form of energy that produces heal, light, and power.]is produced in power plants and carried to your home via the electric power grid.

Nonrenewable Energy Resources

Nonrenewableenergy sources are those that exist in a limited supply and cannot be replaced or replenished as quickly as they are used. There are two main types of nonrenewable energy sources:

  • 1. Fossil Fuels:Fossil fuels(coal, petroleum/crude oil, and natural gas)formed from the buried remains of plants and animals that lived millions of years ago.
  • 2. Nuclear Energy:Nuclear power is energy in the nucleus (core) of an atom. Splitting atoms produces heat, which makes electricity, in a number electricity, in a nuclear power plant.

Renewable Energy Resources

The most common renewable energy sources are

  1. Hydroelectric power, or hydropower, ueses flowing water to turn turbines to produce electricity.
  2. Solar energy uses a variety of technologies to convert sunlight to usable energy. Energy from the sun can be used to heat water, heat or cool air, and generate electricity for individual homes or buildings. On a large scale, it can be used to produce electricity to power cities and small towns.
  3. Wind energy uses air blowing across propeller blades causing them to spin to produce electricity.

電力

電力[註：電力是一種能產生熱能、光能及動力的能源。]是在發電廠生產，並透過電力網輸送到您家中的。

不可再生能源資源

不可再生能源是指儲量有限，且無法像消耗速度那樣迅速被取代或補充的能源。不可再生能源主要有兩種類型：

  • 1. 化石燃料：化石燃料(煤炭、石油／原油及天然氣)是由數百萬年前的動植物遺骸經埋藏後形成的。
  • 2. 核能：核能是原子核(核心)中所蘊含的能量。在核電廠中，透過分裂原子所產生的熱能，進而產生電力。

再生能源資源

最常見的再生能源來源是

  1. 水力發電，或 水電 , 利用流動的水轉動渦輪機來發電。
  2. 太陽能 運用多種技術，將陽光轉化為可用能源。 太陽能可用於加熱水、調節空氣溫度（供暖或製冷），以及為個別住宅或建築物發電。 若大規模應用，則可發電以供應城市及小鎮的電力需求。
  3. 風力發電 利用吹過葉片的風力, 使葉片旋轉以產生電力。
